Note:
The Internal Screening Committee evaluated the candidature of Col. (Retd.) Rajesh Sharma
for the position of Registrar at Model Institute of Engineering & Technology.
The Committee noted that Col. (Retd.) Rajesh Sharma is a distinguished Indian Army veteran
with over 30 years of experience in senior operational, administrative, human resource
management, project management, security and institutional leadership roles. His academic
qualifications include B.E Electronics and Advanced Management program from OP Jindal
University. In addition to this he has undertaken several courses including Young Officers
Course, Graduate Engineering Entry Course, Company Commander Courses, Regimental
Command Course (from MCTE, Mhow), Junior Command Course and Senior Command
Course (from Army War College)
After carefully considering extensive leadership experience, proven administrative capabilities
and the relevance of his expertise to the governance and administration of a higher
educational institution, the Committee is of the considered opinion that he is well suited for
the position of Registrar.
Accordingly, matter is placed before the Governing Body to ratify the appointment of Col.
(Retd.) Rajesh Sharma as Registrar.

Item No. 6
To report on the status of commencement of Ph.D. in Engineering programs at MIET.
Note:
The members were apprised of the approval accorded by the University of Jammu for the
commencement of the Ph.D. in Engineering programs at MIET from the Academic Session
2026–27, the University of Jammu Notification is attached as Annexure - V.
As per the University guidelines, the Ph.D. programs shall be administered through a
structured research framework. Doctoral research shall be overseen by the Board of Research
Studies (BORS) at the University level, while the Institute shall constitute DRCs and RACs to
guide and monitor research scholars. Eligible regular faculty members of the Institute shall
serve as supervisors, with co-supervisors from the University of Jammu being associated,
wherever required.
Admissions to the Ph.D. programs shall be conducted in accordance with the regulations of
the University of Jammu and UGC norms. The Institute shall notify available research seats
and approved supervisors, conduct course work, facilitate scholar registration, and monitor
research progress as per prescribed guidelines.
The Institute shall also ensure compliance with University regulations relating to research
supervision, progress review, thesis submission, evaluation, and viva-voce examinations,
while maintaining the necessary academic and administrative support for doctoral studies.
