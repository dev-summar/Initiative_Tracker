Executive Summary
The Model Institute of Engineering and Technology (MIET) envisions transforming into a world-class
institution that delivers exceptional value to its students, industry partners, and society. This strategic
plan for 2024-2030 outlines MIET's approach to navigating the rapidly changing landscape of higher
education while addressing the disruptions in the global economy and job markets caused by
technological advancements.
Over the next five years, MIET will focus on key areas, including enhancing academic excellence,
advancing technological innovation, expanding infrastructure, and promoting sustainability. The
institution will leverage its core values of Excellence, Diligence, and Innovation to maintain its
leadership in higher education, ensuring that it not only adapts to future challenges but also contributes
actively to shaping them.
MIET's strategic plan revolves around Core Values, Key Focus Areas, Transformational Strategies,
Enabling Strategies, and the in-house developed SOUL (Strategy, Operations, Urgency and Leadership)
framework for institutional transformation. SOUL further encompasses the Simplified Strategic
Management Framework (SSMF) a 5-dimensional model outlining key focus areas and specific
interventions leading to increasing maturity levels indicative of stakeholder delight, higher order
outcomes and sustained brand strengthening. Detailed descriptions of these strategic elements are
provided below.
Core Values
MIET's core values of “Shreshtha” (Excellence), “Shram” (Diligence), and “Navinta” (Innovation)
form the foundation of its mission and strategic approach. Excellence drives the pursuit of high
standards, Diligence emphasizes commitment and consistency of execution, and Innovation encourages
forward-thinking solutions to evolving challenges. These values have remained unchanged over the 90
year existence of the MIER group and continue to inspire the next leg of transformative development of
MIET and other group institutions.
Key Focus Areas
Enhance Student Achievement: MIET aims to equip students with industry-relevant skills and
knowledge, delivering a world-class educational experience. The focus will be on supporting
students in gaining admissions to prestigious institutions and securing placements in top
companies, thus aligning with the institution's commitment to excellence.
Build Networks and Partnerships: Strategic partnerships with industry and like-minded
institutions will be developed to create more training, research, and employment opportunities.
Strengthening the alumni network will also be crucial for ongoing engagement and support.
Promote Local Innovation: MIET will institutionalize a culture of innovation with a focus on
addressing real-world problems, particularly those relevant to the local context. By enhancing
research capabilities and encouraging student-driven innovation, MIET will position itself as a
hub for inventive solutions.

Champion IT Adoption: To drive operational efficiency and improve the student experience,
MIET will advance its IT infrastructure and develop innovative in-house solutions. The
institution will also foster a technologically advanced culture, supporting faculty and student
research and innovation through targeted initiatives.
Transformational Strategies
MIET's approach to transformation includes four key strategies: Quality, Innovation, Blue Ocean, and
Customer Experience.
Quality: A commitment to high standards will be reinforced through continuous improvement,
accreditation, and quality certifications, ensuring transparency and alignment with institutional
goals.
Innovation: MIET will promote a culture of innovation, encouraging student-driven projects
and enhancing research capabilities to address real-world challenges.
Customer Experience: Creating a world-class experience for students through engagement and
satisfaction-focused initiatives will be a priority, ensuring MIET stands out as a leader in student
services.
Blue Ocean: By embracing non-traditional approaches and creating new opportunities, MIET
will differentiate itself within the higher education sector.
Enabling Strategies
To support its transformational goals, MIET will focus on Professionalism, Performance, and
Responsiveness:
Professionalism: Sustained training and modern management techniques will foster
professionalism across the institution.
Responsiveness: MIET will cultivate an agile, stakeholder-focused environment, ensuring the
institution remains adaptable and proactive in meeting the needs of its community.
Performance: A data-driven approach will enhance organizational performance, aligning staff
and student capabilities with strategic objectives.
Institutional Transformation
Around 10 years ago, the institutional leadership recognized large trends playing out in the Indian Higher
Education space, namely, increased student expectations, regulatory changes necessitating urgent
transformation, focus on accreditation and rankings, heightened competition and existential concerns
for private affiliated colleges. Recognizing these trends, the institution decided to adopt a proactive
approach focusing on “student value creation” and emerging as the de facto leader in the region. This

Key Components of the SOUL Framework are

mission with its strategic goals. This component emphasizes creating a clear and adaptable plan
that addresses both immediate and long-term challenges while integrating global best practices.
Operations(O): Operational excellence is vital for enhancing stakeholder experience and
institutional efficiency. This involves optimizing processes, leveraging technology, and building
strong support systems to ensure seamless operations. Improving operational efficiency at MIET
shall empower faculty and staff to deliver high-quality education and services.
Urgency(U): A sense of urgency is crucial for driving timely change within MIET. By fostering a
culture of urgency, MIET must accelerate decision-making and execution, achieve quick wins,
and maintain momentum in its transformation journey. This component is essential for
overcoming resistance to change and ensuring sustained progress.
Leadership(L): Transformational leadership is required to guide MIET through change
effectively. This involves developing strong leaders at all levels who can articulate a clear vision,

strategic management at MIET. It is designed to be action-oriented and easily assimilated at all levels in
the organization. The SSMF depicted below in Figure 2 is structured around five key dimensions:
Students, Faculty, Information Technology, Resources, and Outreach & Branding. Each dimension has
four maturity levels, which shall help MIET track its progress and gauge the maturity of different
strategic management initiatives.

Building on the foundations of previous strategic plans, annual objectives, and the strategy document,
MIET embarked on a collaborative journey to define its path for the next five years. A series of in-depth
discussions were conducted, engaging stakeholders from various levels of the institution to ensure a
comprehensive approach resulting in the Strategic Planning Dossier (Appendix I).These
conversations culminated in a strategic retreat where the core leadership group came together to
contribute their insights and expertise. This collective effort allowed MIET to align its vision with
actionable steps, laying the groundwork for a transformative strategy roadmap that addresses both
current and future challenges.
The strategic plan has been designed to attain the most important strategic outcome for MIET,
which is “to evolve into a Private University” over the next 5 years.
As a result of these discussions, the MIET Strategic Plan 2024-2030 emerged, focusing on five key
objectives that will elevate the stature of the institution. These objectives target critical areas: academic
and research excellence, technological innovation, infrastructure development, sustainability, and
enhancing the holistic experience of students, faculty, and stakeholders. By centring on these priorities,
MIET intends not only expand its impact but also help drive socio-economic progress in the region and
contribute to national development. The five strategic objectives, along with their corresponding SSMF

Technological Advancement and Digital Transformation
MIET is committed to enhancing its digital infrastructure to support its strategic goals of becoming a
world-class institution. The upcoming five years will focus on accelerating MIET's technology adpotion
through modernized infrastructure, implementation of emerging technologies, and creating a digital-
first operational model. These efforts aim to elevate academic delivery, institutional efficiency, and foster
innovation, making MIET a leader in technology adoption and advancement.
Digital Infrastructure Modernization
Upgrading Network Capabilities: MIET will invest in high-speed internet, upgrading to 1 Gbps
bandwidth to support data-intensive research and remote learning. High-density Wi-Fi networks
will ensure seamless connectivity across academic buildings, residential areas, and public
spaces. A redundant network architecture will minimize downtime, ensuring uninterrupted
connectivity.
Cloud-First Strategy: The adoption of a cloud-first strategy will enhance data management and
scalability. Cloud platforms like AWS or Microsoft Azure will host institutional data and digital
resources. Tools like Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 will facilitate real-time
collaboration, while virtual labs will provide remote access to high-performance computing
resources.
Enhancing Academic Delivery through Technology
Smart Classrooms: MIET will transform its classrooms into smart learning environments
equipped with interactive displays and AI-powered tools. Lecture capture systems will enable
recording and streaming of lectures, creating a repository for review. AI tools will provide
personalized feedback and support to students.
Learning Management System (LMS) Enhancement: The LMS will be upgraded to offer a
user-friendly interface and mobile access, supporting all academic activities. Integrated
assessment tools will track student progress, providing analytics to help faculty address learning
gaps effectively.
Virtual and Augmented Reality in Education: MIET will incorporate VR and AR technologies
for immersive learning where applicable.

Promoting Sustainability and Social Responsibility
The Model Institute of Engineering and Technology (MIET) is dedicated to aligning its initiatives with
the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (UNSDGs) as part of its 2024-2030 strategic plan.
This commitment aims to integrate sustainability, social equity, and community engagement across
MIET's operations, academic programs, and research, ensuring the institution contributes positively to
global challenges like climate change, inequality, and sustainable development.
Sustainability in Campus Operations
MIET integrates sustainable practices in campus operations, targeting energy usage, waste
management, water conservation, and transportation, aligning with UNSDGs such as Affordable and
Clean Energy (Goal 7), Responsible Consumption and Production (Goal 12), and Climate Action (Goal
13).
Energy Efficiency and Clean Energy Initiatives
Solar Energy Deployment: MIET plans to meet 75% of its energy needs through solar
power by 2030, installing solar panels across academic buildings and open spaces. On-
campus solar farms will enhance energy generation, complemented by battery storage
systems to manage energy use during peak times.
Energy Efficiency Upgrades: Smart energy management systems will optimize energy
consumption based on real-time data, while retrofitting existing buildings with LED
lighting, improved insulation, and energy-efficient appliances will further reduce energy
use.
Sustainable Water Management
Rainwater Harvesting: Systems will collect and store rainwater for irrigation and other
non-potable uses, reducing the need for municipal water and supporting groundwater
recharge. Greywater recycling will also be implemented to further decrease reliance on
fresh water.
Water-Efficient Landscaping: MIET will adopt landscaping practices that use native
and drought-tolerant plants, alongside smart irrigation systems to minimize water waste
and enhance campus biodiversity.
Waste Management and Circular Economy Practices
Zero-Waste Initiatives: MIET will promote sustainable consumption, reduce single-use
materials, and encourage digital workflows to minimize paper use. Comprehensive
recycling and composting programs will be established to divert waste from landfills and
recycle organic materials for use in campus gardens.
